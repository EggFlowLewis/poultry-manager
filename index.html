Frequently asked questions

Built for commercial UK poultry farms.

How is my farm data secured?

The platform is designed around farm-level access controls and separate staff permissions. Security and retention policies will be confirmed before launch.

Can staff use it on mobile or offline in sheds?

The interface is mobile-friendly. Offline record capture and synchronisation are planned; availability will be confirmed before launch.

How do I add stockpersons?

Commercial Pro is designed for unlimited stockperson logins, with unlimited staff on Enterprise. Owners will manage permissions for each account.

What formats are available for Red Tractor and DEFRA records?

Inspection packs are intended to be downloadable PDFs, organised for review and printing. Farms remain responsible for checking current record requirements.

About Poultry Manager UK

Poultry Manager UK (poultrymanager.uk) is cloud-based flock performance and compliance management software designed specifically for UK commercial broiler growers and multi-shed poultry farms. Replace shed clipboards with digital FCR and EPEF tracking, feed silo forecasting, mobile staff check-ins, biosecurity records and inspection reports supporting Red Tractor, APHA and DEFRA requirements.
